A big update. Tidalfast uses much less memory as songs go by, the queue and History keep exactly what you expect, the player bar looks like TIDAL's, and themes and plugins match TIDAL 2.47.0 with TidaLuna 1.20 more closely and run faster.

**New**
- **The player bar is TIDAL's.** TIDAL's own icons, spacing and layout, with or without a theme, and it adapts to the window's width as TIDAL's does.
- **Explore and Home show TIDAL's featured promotions,** such as TIDAL Magazine articles and TIDAL pages, at TIDAL's sizes.
- **Folders in the sidebar** look like TIDAL's when rolled up.
- **Lyrics, similar artists and video suggestions come from TIDAL's newer service,** as in TIDAL itself, so you get the same lists TIDAL shows (20 video suggestions instead of 10).
- **Plugins can use TIDAL's official API** on your behalf if you allow it, with a permission of its own that is off until you turn it on.
- **TidaLuna 1.20's plugin stores,** including meowarex's store at its new address. Plugins you installed from the old addresses move over by themselves.

**Fixed**
- **Much less memory over a long listening session.** Memory no longer keeps growing as songs change, especially on Windows.
- **History keeps its songs.** A song that came round again in a shuffle no longer empties History, and nor does playing a song from a sorted or filtered list of what is playing.
- **A resumed shuffle finishes what is left** instead of replaying songs you already heard.
- **A playlist started from a card on Home or Explore** shows its name in the queue, not just "Playlist".
- **Lyrics keep up** right after you skip, seek or pause.
- **Playlists:** a new playlist shows its cover at once, a saved name or description shows straight away, and you can clear a description.
- **Right-to-left text** (Arabic, Hebrew) wraps and edits correctly.
- **Themes match TIDAL much more closely:** headers and their cover art, the player bar, the sidebar and its glass, menus, the search field, rounded corners, animations and hover effects.
- **Plugins work closer to TidaLuna** on artist pages, search, library rows, albums and lyrics, and turning one plugin off no longer stops others from hearing the player.
- **Faster with plugins and themes on.** Scrolling and hovering do far less work, and a paused Home with many plugins uses about a third of the processor it did.

**Thanks**
