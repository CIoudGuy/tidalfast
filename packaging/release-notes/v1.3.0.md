Less memory, a steadier queue and History, TIDAL's player bar, and closer themes and plugins.

**New**
- **TIDAL's player bar,** with or without a theme.
- **Featured promotions** on Home and Explore.
- **Sidebar folders** look like TIDAL's.
- **Lyrics, similar artists and video suggestions** from TIDAL's newer service.
- **Plugins can use TIDAL's official API** if you allow it.
- **TidaLuna 1.20's plugin stores.**

**Fixed**
- **Much less memory** over long sessions.
- **History keeps its songs.**
- **A resumed shuffle** plays only what is left.
- **Home and Explore playlists** show their name in the queue.
- **Lyrics keep up** after skip, seek or pause.
- **Playlist covers, names and descriptions** update at once.
- **Right-to-left text** wraps and edits correctly.
- **Themes and plugins** match TIDAL and TidaLuna more closely, and run faster.

- Inrixia and the TidaLuna contributors, and every plugin and theme author.
