A big update: TidaLuna plugins and themes now look and work much more like they do in TIDAL, playback is steadier, and the app is lighter and safer.

**New**
- **Plugins work like in TidaLuna.** Many more plugins and themes now match TIDAL with TidaLuna: Tidal Tags, radiant-lyrics, tidal-genres, CoverTheme, StarryNight, 3D Cover, the audio visualiser and more.
- **52 themes in the store,** including Tidal TUI, Tidal Light, Rosé Pine, Catppuccin and every Cotton Candy colourway. A theme made for Light or Dark mode says so.
- **Plugin permissions.** A plugin can ask for your TIDAL sign-in, system-wide media keys, saving TIDAL audio or running a program. Each is off until you allow it, and you can take it back.
- **Reset to defaults** on each plugin, and removing a plugin now deletes everything it saved, models included.
- **Explicit albums** show their E badge, and artist pages list TIDAL's own versions, including Live albums.
- **Narrow windows look like TIDAL.** Song lists drop columns instead of squeezing, and the page sits beside the play queue.

**Fixed**
- **Songs no longer skip when your internet drops.** They wait and carry on when you're back online.
- **A double-clicked song starts at once,** even far down a long playlist, and plays from the row you clicked.
- **No more crashes** when closing the app during a request, or when a plugin used its own font.
- **Lighter on your PC.** Plugins rest while the window is covered, and now playing uses far less CPU.
- **Security fixes** for plugins, links and damaged files.
- **Discord status** shows the song that is actually playing.

**Thanks**
- Inrixia and the TidaLuna contributors, and every plugin and theme author.
