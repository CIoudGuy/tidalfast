Plugins and themes closer to TIDAL, steadier playback, and a lighter app.

**New**
- **More plugins work as in TidaLuna.**
- **52 themes in the store.**
- **Plugin permissions,** off until you allow them.
- **Reset a plugin** to its defaults.
- **Explicit badges** and TIDAL's album versions on artist pages.
- **Narrow windows** look like TIDAL.

**Fixed**
- **No skips** when your internet drops.
- **Double-clicked songs** start at once.
- **Fewer crashes** on close and with plugin fonts.
- **Less CPU** use.
- **Security fixes** for plugins, links and damaged files.
- **Discord status** shows the right song.

**Thanks**
- Inrixia and the TidaLuna contributors, and every plugin and theme author.
