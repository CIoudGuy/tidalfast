A dev build that brings themes closer to TIDAL and lets plugins hide parts of song rows.

**Fixed**
- **Hidden song details stay hidden.** When a plugin such as element-hider hides a song's title, artist, album or duration, it is gone from the row.
- **Theme sizes match TIDAL.** Sizes themes give in `rem` now use TIDAL's 14 px text size, so spacing and text are no longer slightly too large.
- **The play queue keeps TIDAL's grey glass** under themes, laid over the page as in TIDAL.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely:** the playing song's row, track titles, full-height header art, mix titles, filter fields and their outline, the playlist picker's and Plugins page's search fields, the sidebar's create menu, and the panel round a playlist's songs.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
