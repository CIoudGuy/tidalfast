Plugin downloads to a picked folder, themes closer to TIDAL, and playback and layout fixes.

**Fixed**
- **DownloadManager** saves to your picked folder.
- **Plugin settings** show changes at once.
- **Tidal Tags** sees the quality at every song change.
- **Next and Previous** move on from the clicked song.
- **Compact mode** keeps window size and place when zoomed.
- **Search pills** scroll sideways in narrow windows.
- **The playing playlist** is TIDAL's green-teal.
- **Themes match TIDAL** more closely.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
