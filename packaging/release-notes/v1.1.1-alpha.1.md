A dev build with fixes on top of 1.1.0: plugin downloads to a picked folder, themes closer to TIDAL, and a few playback and layout fixes.

**Fixed**
- **DownloadManager** saves to the folder you pick, instead of asking every time with "Download folder unavailable".
- **Plugin settings** show a ticked box or a picked choice at once.
- **Tidal Tags** and other plugins see the playing song's quality at every song change.
- **Next and Previous** right after you click a song move on from that song, with Repeat all on too.
- **Compact mode** keeps the window's size and place when the interface is zoomed.
- **Search's filter pills** stay on one row and scroll sideways in a narrow window, as in TIDAL.
- **The playing playlist** in the sidebar is TIDAL's green-teal.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely:** page titles, the play queue's playing song, navigation rows, Explore's pills, home shortcuts and the back and forward buttons.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
