A dev build with queue fixes, a player bar closer to TIDAL's, and themes and plugins that match TIDAL 2.47.0 with TidaLuna 1.20 more closely and run faster.

**Fixed**
- **History keeps its songs.** A song that came round again in a shuffle was taken for a step back, and History lost everything after it. Playing a song from a sorted or filtered list of what is already playing no longer empties History either.
- **A resumed shuffle finishes what is left.** After a restart, songs you had already heard no longer play again with Repeat off. Repeat all still goes round the whole album or playlist.

**New**
- **The player bar is closer to TIDAL's,** with or without a theme: TIDAL's own icons, its spacing, and the buttons on the right laid out as TIDAL lays them out.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely:** animated stars, scaling and hover effects, album rows, the pinned album header, library rows and the see-through context menu.
- **Plugins reach more of the app.** The artist page, its Top Tracks and shelves, search filters and top results, and library rows now hold TIDAL's own elements, so plugins and themes that style them work there.

**Faster**
- **Much less work with plugins on.** A paused Home with 18 plugins uses about a third of the processor it did, and scrolling a long playlist styles new rows in under half the time. Hovering restyles only what the hover changes.
