A dev build that brings themes and plugins closer still to TIDAL with TidaLuna, and fixes songs added to the queue showing without their cover.

**Fixed**
- **Added songs keep their cover and artists.** A song added to Playing next from an artist page or anywhere else now shows its whole row in the queue, and keeps it when the queue is refilled.
- **Plugins work more like in TidaLuna.** A plugin's settings open only while its page shows them, as in TidaLuna, so plugins such as playlist-tools no longer misbehave. Plugins can now give a song words that show in the app, switch the now-playing view, add to the Collection menu and read the quality badge as TIDAL shows it, also for a song playing on another device.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely:** My Collection's pages and cards, the player bar's song card and glass, the sidebar's cards, a play queue a theme opens over the page, headers, menus and backgrounds. Sizes a theme gives relative to the window now follow the window.
- **Faster with themes on.** Scrolling a long queue under a theme is several times smoother.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
