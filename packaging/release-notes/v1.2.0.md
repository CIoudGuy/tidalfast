A big update. Plugins and themes look and work much closer to how they do in TIDAL with TidaLuna, the app is much faster with them on, plugins can open their own windows, and the queue keeps exactly what you put in it.

**New**
- **Plugin windows.** Plugins with a window of their own, like miniplayer and lyrics-everywhere, open it as a real window, see-through around its edges on Windows.
- **Feed badge.** The sidebar's Feed row shows how many new updates are waiting, as in TIDAL, and opening Feed marks them as seen.
- **Plugin fonts and colours.** Plugins and themes can load their own web fonts and use every CSS colour, so radiant-lyrics and others look the way their authors made them.
- **TidaLuna 1.18's plugin store.** The plugin store now lists the same stores as TidaLuna 1.18, including TidalPresence for your Discord status.
- **Lyrics for plugins.** Lyric plugins get lyrics even for songs TIDAL has none for, the same ones the Lyrics tab shows.

**Fixed**
- **The queue keeps what you queue.** Songs you queued no longer come back after they played or were removed, and a song you add shows its cover and artists right away, whichever page it came from.
- **Next and Previous** right after you click a song move on from that song, with Repeat all on too.
- **Much faster with plugins and themes on.** Long playlists and queues scroll many times more smoothly, and the app uses less CPU while idle and while plugins animate.
- **Sharper text and drawing on Windows.** Text is as crisp as in TIDAL, shadows are softer and rounded corners exact.
- **Themes match TIDAL much more closely** almost everywhere: page backgrounds and frosted glass, headers and titles, menus, the sidebar, the search field and back and forward buttons, filter pills, the player bar, the play queue, My Collection and the whole Cotton Candy family. Theme sizes now use TIDAL's text size and follow the window's size.
- **Plugins work closer to TidaLuna.** They can read, rewrite, hide and swap the pictures of many more parts of the app. radiant-lyrics follows the song smoothly, lyric plugins are in time with the music, the audio visualiser reads the sound correctly, and Tidal Tags, element-hider, uwu-ifier, star-ratings, playlist-tools, betterFullscreen, korean-translation and others behave much more like they do in TIDAL. Not every plugin is there yet, and work on them continues.
- **Plugin settings** open only on their own page, as in TidaLuna, and a ticked box or a picked choice shows at once.
- **Right-clicks** reach plugins first, and a plugin that cancels one stops the app's menu, as in TIDAL.
- **The player bar's song title** shows in full next to plugin ratings and genre chips.
- **DownloadManager** saves to the folder you pick, and large plugin downloads are no longer lost when the computer is busy.
- **Compact mode** keeps the window's size and place when the interface is zoomed.
- **Search's filter pills** stay on one row in a narrow window, as in TIDAL.
