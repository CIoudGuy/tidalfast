Closer, faster plugins and themes, plugin windows, and a queue that keeps what you put in it.

**New**
- **Plugin windows** open as real windows.
- **Feed badge** shows new updates.
- **Plugin fonts and colours** work as their authors made them.
- **TidaLuna 1.18's plugin store.**
- **Lyrics for plugins,** the same ones the Lyrics tab shows.

**Fixed**
- **The queue keeps what you queue.**
- **Next and Previous** after clicking a song.
- **Much faster** with plugins and themes on.
- **Sharper text and drawing** on Windows.
- **Themes and plugins** match TIDAL and TidaLuna much more closely.
- **Plugin settings, right-clicks and downloads** behave as in TidaLuna.
- **Compact mode and narrow windows** keep their layout.

**Thanks**
- Inrixia and the TidaLuna contributors, and every plugin and theme author.
