A dev build with many fixes: songs you click start at once, no more skipping when the internet drops, explicit albums, and plugins and themes closer to TidaLuna.

**New**
- **Reset to defaults** on each plugin's card clears its settings and starts it fresh.
- **Narrow windows look like TIDAL.** Song lists drop columns instead of squeezing, headers stack, and the page sits beside the open play queue.
- **Live albums** on artist pages, as in TIDAL.
- **The audio visualiser works** again (it needs ffmpeg installed and asks before running it).
- **DownloadManager** can be allowed behind the save-audio permission, off until you allow it.

**Fixed**
- **A crash** when a plugin used its own font after the window was reopened.
- **Songs no longer skip when your internet drops.** Songs and music videos wait and carry on when you're back online.
- **A double-clicked song starts at once,** even far down a long playlist, and a song listed twice plays from the row you clicked. Next, Previous, Play and Shuffle pressed while a song loads now act on that song.
- **Explicit albums** show their E badge, and artist pages list TIDAL's own versions in TIDAL's order.
- **Discord status** shows the song that is actually playing.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely,** including Rosé Pine, Catppuccin, Tidal Light, Tidal TUI, Sunken Tidal and the Cotton Candy family.
- **Plugins match TidaLuna more closely,** including radiant-lyrics, tidal-genres, quick-queue, CoverTheme, StarryNight and DownloadManager, and they see layout changes right away.
- **The album review** is only on the Credits page now.
- **Translations** for the newest text.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
