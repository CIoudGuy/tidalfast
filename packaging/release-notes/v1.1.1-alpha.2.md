A dev build focused on plugins and themes: radiant-lyrics and other lyric plugins work in motion as in TidaLuna, and themes reach many more parts of the app.

**New**
- **Web fonts for plugins and themes.** A plugin's own font from the web loads, so radiant-lyrics' lyrics use its own typeface.
- **Every CSS colour.** Themes and plugins can use all named colours and the newer colour functions, and colour mixing matches the browser.

**Fixed**
- **radiant-lyrics follows the song.** Its lines no longer flicker, they scroll with the song and slide up, the sung word fills in, and the lines away from the sung one are blurred as in TIDAL.
- **Lyric plugins are in time** with the Lyrics tab (they ran about two seconds early) and get lyrics even when TIDAL has none.
- **star-ratings** syncs to its Rated playlist again.
- **The player bar's title** shows in full next to plugin ratings and genre chips.
- **The audio visualiser** no longer blinks on quiet songs.
- **Turning covers and other endless plugin animations** use far less CPU.
- **Themes match TIDAL more closely:** menus (colours, borders, hover, icons and submenus), the sidebar's pills and glow, the search pill, the Play and Shuffle buttons, Explore, the player bar's cards, the play queue's rows and links, track titles, and the Collection grid's glass panel.
- **The play queue's artist names** are TIDAL's grey, followed artists included.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
