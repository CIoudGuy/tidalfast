The first public release of Tidalfast: a native desktop client for TIDAL on Windows and Linux, written in Rust with no browser inside.

**New**
- **Lossless and Max.** 16-bit FLAC and 24-bit hi-res, the streams TIDAL serves, decoded bit-exact. Log in with Max for hi-res.
- **Bit-perfect output.** Optional exclusive mode (WASAPI on Windows, ALSA on Linux) hands your DAC each track at its own rate.
- **Fast and light.** Opens in under a second and uses about 120 MB of memory.
- **Feels like TIDAL.** Home, Explore, Feed, Collection, and now playing with lyrics, credits and similar tracks.
- **Music videos.** Mixed into the queue with songs, with picture-in-picture, up to 1080p.
- **Offline.** Download albums, playlists and mixes. Offline mode plays only your downloads and makes no requests to TIDAL.
- **TIDAL Upload.** Upload your own music and edit your releases.
- **My Activity.** Your monthly top artists from TIDAL, plus what you played in Tidalfast.
- **TidaLuna plugins.** Optional, off by default. Install plugins and themes from the Luna stores.
- **16 languages.** The interface follows your system's language, or the one you pick in Settings.

Which file to download is under [Install](https://github.com/CIoudGuy/tidalfast#install) in the README. You need a TIDAL subscription to play music.
