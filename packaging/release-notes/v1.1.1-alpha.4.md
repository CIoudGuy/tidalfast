A big dev build: plugins and themes look and work much more like they do in TIDAL with TidaLuna, and the app is much faster with plugins on.

**New**
- **Plugin windows.** A plugin that opens its own window, like miniplayer or lyrics-everywhere, now opens it as a real window, see-through around its edges on Windows.
- **Feed badge.** The sidebar's Feed row shows how many new updates there are, as in TIDAL, and opening Feed marks them as seen.

**Fixed**
- **Much faster with plugins on.** Scrolling long playlists and queues with plugins or a theme on is many times faster, and the app uses less CPU while idle.
- **Sharper and smoother drawing.** Text rows are crisper, like TIDAL's, shadows are softer and rounded corners exact.
- **Themes match TIDAL much more closely:** page backgrounds and gradients, frosted glass, the player bar's glass, the play queue, menus, headers and the Cotton Candy family's layout.
- **Plugins work more like in TidaLuna.** They can read, rewrite, hide and swap the pictures of many more parts of the page: song rows, the play queue, Home's cards and shelves, headers, buttons and covers. Plugins such as uwu-ifier, korean-translation, element-hider, betterFullscreen, Avatar and total-play-time now behave as in TIDAL.
- **Right-clicks** reach plugins before the menu opens, and a plugin that cancels one stops the app's menu, as in TIDAL.
- **Large plugin downloads** are no longer lost when the computer is busy or plugins restart.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
