A big dev build: TidaLuna plugins and themes matched against the real TidaLuna, a TIDAL-like look, and new plugin permissions.

**New**
- **Plugins work like in TidaLuna.** Tidal Tags, LuckyLP, star-ratings, CatJam, element-hider, up-next, tidal-genres, qr-share, copy-lyrics and many more now look and behave as they do in TIDAL with TidaLuna.
- **Plugin permissions.** A plugin can ask for your TIDAL sign-in, system-wide media keys, another program's pipe, or saving TIDAL audio. Each is off until you allow it, once or always, and you can take it back in the plugin's Permissions.
- **3D Cover.** The new @visiuun/3d-cover plugin works: hover the now-playing cover for a 3D effect from an AI depth map made on your computer.
- **52 themes in the store,** including Tidal TUI, Tidal Light and every Cotton Candy colourway.
- **Plugin animations.** Fades, spinners and other plugin and theme animations play.
- **Settings in TidaLuna's look.** A plugin's settings use TidaLuna's switches, number steppers, dropdowns and buttons.
- **Closer to TIDAL.** Home, album pages, the play queue, search, settings and now playing look more like TIDAL's.

**Fixed**
- **Playing from after a playlist ends.** When autoplay carries on, the queue now says it plays from the last song's radio instead of the finished playlist.
- **Links in What's new** open when clicked.

Tidalfast is an independent project and is not affiliated with TIDAL.
